              What was wrong and how it was fixed

              Emails are partly hidden; ␣ marks a stray space. Passwords are never read.

              Rules

              • Null / missing (completeness): name, email, order id, item quantity, price or product is blank.
              • Duplicate (uniqueness): the same customer twice (name or email equal after trimming and lower-casing), or the same order id stored twice. The first registration is kept; the duplicate's orders move to it.
              • Inconsistent / invalid (consistency, validity): stray spaces or capitals, an email that is not a valid address, an unknown product, a quantity that is not a whole number above 0.
              • Discrepancy (accuracy): price differs from the catalogue, subtotal ≠ price × qty, total ≠ sum of subtotals, tax ≠ total − total ÷ 1.2 (the shop's 20 % included tax).
              • Fix policy: only changes that need no guess are applied (formats, merges, recomputed amounts, doubled "@" or "." typos). Anything else is flagged needs_review for a human. A backup of every affected record is saved before writing.

              AI findings report

              Planner → Analyzer → Recommender agents over the measured runs. Python computes every number; the agents explain, rank and recommend.

              Ranked weaknesses

              Severity = 0.4 × error spike (worse of overall errors and failed checkouts) + 0.3 × incident time + 0.3 × cascade, + up to 20 for slowdowns.
